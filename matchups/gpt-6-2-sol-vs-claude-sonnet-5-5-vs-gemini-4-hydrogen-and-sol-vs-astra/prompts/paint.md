# Paint: the prompt

Read RULES.md in this folder. Write the strongest bot you can for that game and save it as bot.js here. You will play a 15-round match against bots written by two other AI models; you will not see them. You can test yours: `node run-match.mjs bot.js practice/wanderer.js practice/still.js --names You,W,S --out test-run` plays a full match against two practice bots (one wanders, one never moves) and prints the score. Finish within 25 minutes. Work offline: do not search the web or open web pages.

Each model also had RULES.md, the arena and two practice bots (one wanders, one never moves) in its folder: [arena/paint/](../arena/paint/).
