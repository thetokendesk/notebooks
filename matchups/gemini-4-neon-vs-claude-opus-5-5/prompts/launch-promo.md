# Product launch promo video: the prompts as sent

1. Make a 20 to 30 second vertical promo video for the launch of Kestrine Cold Brew, for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Use our photos and logo (brand/) and the launch brief (inputs/launch-brief.md). No music or voice-over is provided, so the words on screen have to carry it. Save it as promo.mp4 (1080x1920).
   
   Everything you need is in this folder: `inputs/`, `brand/` (the brand guide, logos and photos). TOOLBOX.md says what is installed; commands here have no network. Work offline: do not search the web or open web pages. GUIDE.md is a guide for this kind of work: follow it. Nobody will answer questions while you work: make sensible choices and note them in NOTES.md. You have 35 minutes. Save the result as `promo.mp4`. Check it with `preview` before you finish.
