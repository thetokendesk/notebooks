Who makes it

We take our time, then commit.

Kestrine is named after the kestrel, the small falcon that hovers in place before it dives. Cold brew suits us. Ours steeps for 18 hours.

Maya Okafor and Tom Reyes started Kestrine in Chattanooga in 2016. Today we roast about 3,600 kg of coffee a month on one 15 kg roaster on the Southside, and pour it in our three cafés.
