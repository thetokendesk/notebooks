What is in the can

Chocolate, toasted almond, a little cherry.

We did not invent a separate recipe for a can. We took the medium-roast house blend people already order in our cafés—beans from Brazil and Colombia—and steeped it cold for 18 hours.

  • Chocolate
  • Toasted almond
  • A little cherry
  • Nothing added
01 · The blend

Brazil and Colombia

Hover House Blend is roasted medium on our 15 kg roaster on Chattanooga's Southside—the same coffee behind our daily bar drinks.

02 · The steep

18 hours in cold water

Steeping cold and slow brings out cocoa sweetness and toasted almond while keeping the finish clean, smooth, and never bitter.

03 · The can

8 oz, grab and go

Just our coffee and water, sealed cold in an 8 oz can. Keep a 12-pack in your fridge or grab one when there is no time for the line.

Chattanooga roaster

Roasted on the Southside since 2016.

Founded by Maya Okafor and Tom Reyes, Kestrine roasts about 3,600 kg of coffee a month across our cafés and online shop. Cold Brew is our first canned coffee—built for people who already love Hover House Blend and want it cold, wherever the day goes.

Named after the kestrel, the small falcon that hovers in place before it dives: we take our time, then commit. Kestrine Coffee Co. · Chattanooga, Tennessee
