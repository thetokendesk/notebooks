# Notes: Kestrine Cold Brew Launch Page

## Output structure
- `site/index.html` is a single self-contained HTML page with inline CSS and vanilla JavaScript, paired with local brand assets in `site/images/`.
- Generated reproducibly by `build_site.py`, which reads `inputs/launch-brief.md` and `brand/BRAND.md`, copies the brand logos untouched (`logo.png`, `logo-dark.png`, `logo-mark.png`), optimizes the selected brand photos (`cold-brew-cans.jpg`, `roastery.jpg`, `farm-visit.jpg`, `cafe-downtown.jpg`, `airport-cafe.jpg`) as progressive JPEGs, and lints against forbidden phrases, headline exclamation marks, and external URLs.
- Opens cleanly offline directly via `file://` with zero external requests or CDN/font dependencies.

## Design and content choices
- **Brand fidelity & voice:** Followed `brand/BRAND.md` strictly—Georgia Bold for headings and Avenir Next (Regular/Medium/Demi Bold) for body and UI; Kestrel Rust (`#B4532A`) reserved as the focal accent for the hero CTA and waitlist submit button (one accent action per viewport); Roast (`#2B1D16`), Crema (`#F3E9DC`), and Sage (`#7A8B6F`) across surfaces and borders; Sky (`#9DB7C9`) omitted since there are no charts. Avoided exclamation marks in headlines and excluded all forbidden copy terms.
- **Logo placement and clear space:** Used `logo.png` directly on an exact Crema (`#F3E9DC`) header and `logo-dark.png` directly on an exact Roast (`#2B1D16`) footer so both blend seamlessly with zero visible edges, preserving aspect ratio and surrounding clear space greater than the mark height at both desktop and phone widths.
- **Facts strictly from inputs:** All dates, pricing, tasting notes, origin countries, café locations, and company history come directly from `inputs/launch-brief.md` and `brand/BRAND.md`. Derived unit math shown for clarity (`$36` for 12 cans = `$3.00` a can; 20% off `$36` = `$28.80`, or `$2.40` a can, with free shipping). Excluded `bags-lineup.jpg` and `subscription-box.jpg` so product photography matches the canned cold brew offer accurately.
- **Waitlist form (offline-ready):** Added email validation, an optional first-name field, and an optional channel preference (`Online 12-pack`, `Chattanooga cafés`, or `Both`) to help gauge launch demand and tailor the confirmation message. Because this deliverable is a static offline page with no backend endpoint, valid submissions are stored locally in `localStorage` (with a reset option to enter another email) and ready to wire to Kestrine's email/waitlist endpoint on `kestrinecoffee.com` at deployment.
- **Phone-first layout:** Designed so the 390 px mobile first screen fits the header, launch date, headline, tasting summary, full-width primary waitlist button, offer note, and complete cold-brew cans hero photo above the fold without horizontal overflow, with all interactive tap targets at least 44 px tall (`48–56 px` for form controls, buttons, and FAQ toggles).
- **Accessibility & contrast:** Verified WCAG AA/AAA text contrast across all palette pairs (white text `#FFFFFF` on Kestrel Rust `#B4532A` passes 4.99:1 AA; Roast `#2B1D16` and Muted Roast `#58463C` on Crema `#F3E9DC` pass 13.57:1 and 7.43:1 AAA; Deep Sage `#48543F` on Crema passes 6.69:1 AA; Light Sage `#A8BC9B` on Roast passes 8.01:1 AAA). Used semantic landmarks, a skip link, ordered `h1`–`h3` headings, native `<details>` FAQs, and `alt` text on all images.
