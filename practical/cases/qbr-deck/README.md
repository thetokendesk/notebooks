# Quarterly business review deck

The prompt, word for word:

I'm Maya, CEO of Kestrine Coffee Co. Our board meets next Tuesday and I need the Q3 2026 business review deck. Our numbers are in data/, my notes are in inputs/q3-notes.md and our brand is in brand/. Ten to twelve slides: how the quarter went, what's driving it, what worries me, and the three things I'm asking the board to decide. Save it as deck.pptx.

Everything you need is in this folder: `inputs/`, `brand/` (the brand guide, logos and photos), `data/`. TOOLBOX.md says what is installed; commands here have no network. Work offline: do not search the web or open web pages. GUIDE.md is a guide for this kind of work: follow it. Nobody will answer questions while you work: make sensible choices and note them in NOTES.md. You have 35 minutes. Save the result as `deck.pptx`. Check it with `preview` before you finish.

## The checklist

Written before the runs; each item scores 0, 1 or 2.

1. Every number matches the data (Q3 2026 revenue $1,274,171, up 17.5%; gross margin 63.1%; wholesale down 32%; subscription up 70%)
2. Each slide makes one point, with a headline that says it
3. Charts, not tables, and readable from the back of the room
4. The three asks are clear and each has what the board needs to decide
5. Brand applied: colours, Georgia and Avenir Next, the logo, no stretched photos
6. Nothing overflows or overlaps; speaker notes on every slide
