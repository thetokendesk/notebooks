# Product launch promo video

The prompt, word for word:

Make a 20 to 30 second vertical promo video for the launch of Kestrine Cold Brew, for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Use our photos and logo (brand/) and the launch brief (inputs/launch-brief.md). No music or voice-over is provided, so the words on screen have to carry it. Save it as promo.mp4 (1080x1920).

Everything you need is in this folder: `inputs/`, `brand/` (the brand guide, logos and photos). TOOLBOX.md says what is installed; commands here have no network. Work offline: do not search the web or open web pages. GUIDE.md is a guide for this kind of work: follow it. Nobody will answer questions while you work: make sensible choices and note them in NOTES.md. You have 35 minutes. Save the result as `promo.mp4`. Check it with `preview` before you finish.

## The checklist

Written before the runs; each item scores 0, 1 or 2.

1. Hook in the first two seconds
2. What, when, price and where all clear by the end
3. Text readable on a phone and clear of the app's buttons
4. Movement and pacing feel made, not a slideshow
5. Brand applied; photos not stretched
6. Plays everywhere: 1080x1920, H.264, 20 to 30 s
