# Kestrine Cold Brew: what we know so far (Tom and Maya, November 2026)

**The product.** Our Hover House Blend, steeped 18 hours, filtered, in an 8 oz can, nothing added. Refrigerated, shelf
life 120 days. Each can uses about 30 g of roasted coffee (our cost for roasted Hover House is about $15.20 a kg today).
Launch planned for April 2027. We'd start with one flavour (Original) and maybe add Night Shift Decaf in year two.

**Two co-packers quoted:**
- *Blue Ridge Beverage Partners* (Asheville): $0.38 a can to brew, fill and seal; minimum run 25,000 cans; 6-week lead
  time; a $12,000 deposit on signing, credited against the first two runs.
- *Riverbend Co-Pack* (Nashville): $0.61 a can; minimum run 8,000 cans; 3-week lead time; no deposit.
- Either way we supply the coffee. Cans with our printed sleeve cost $0.15 each, minimum order 50,000 sleeves.
- Freight from the co-packer to our cold storage: about $450 a run.

**Where it would sell, and for how much:**
- Our three cafés, $4.50 a can. Our guess: 40 cans a day across the three cafés from May to September, 20 a day the
  rest of the year.
- Online, 12-packs at $36 plus shipping (cold-pack shipping costs us about $11 a box; we charge $9). Guess: 150 boxes in
  the first month, growing about 5% a month.
- Wholesale accounts (offices, restaurants), $2.10 a can. Guess: 500 cans in month one, rising to 2,500 a month by
  month six.
- Three local grocery stores are interested at $2.25 a can to us (they'd sell at $3.99). Not signed. Maybe from month
  four, about 1,500 cans a month together.

**Other costs:**
- Can design and sleeve artwork: $8,000, once.
- Launch marketing: $25,000 over the first three months, then $3,000 a month.
- Cold storage space: $1,200 a month.
- Card fees about 3% on café and online sales.
- A part-time person to run deliveries and stock: $2,600 a month from launch.

**What we don't know:** how much cold brew would take sales from our iced coffee in the cafés (Tom says 30% of cans
sold in cafés would have been an iced coffee anyway, at about the same margin), whether the grocery stores sign,
and whether the 25,000-can minimum leaves us with cans past their 120 days.

Our board wants to see when this pays back and how much cash it needs before it does.
