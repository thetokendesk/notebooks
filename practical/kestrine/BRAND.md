# Kestrine Coffee Co.: brand guide

Kestrine Coffee Co. is a made-up company for our tests. Any likeness to a real business is an accident.

## Who we are

A specialty coffee roaster in Chattanooga, Tennessee, founded in 2016 by Maya Okafor (CEO) and Tom Reyes (head
roaster). Named after the kestrel, the small falcon that hovers in place before it dives: we take our time, then
commit. 41 staff.

- **Roastery:** one 15 kg roaster on the Southside, about 3,600 kg of coffee a month.
- **Cafés:** Downtown (since 2017), North Shore (since 2019), Airport (opened June 2025).
- **Online shop:** whole-bean and ground coffee, brewing gear, merch; ships in the US.
- **Subscription:** a bag every 2 or 4 weeks; the fastest-growing part of the business.
- **Wholesale:** beans for about 30 restaurants and offices. Lost its largest account (a hotel group) in April 2026.
- **Launching in April 2027:** Kestrine Cold Brew in 8 oz cans, sold in the cafés, online and to wholesale accounts.

## Voice

Warm, plain and specific. Say what the coffee tastes like and where it came from, in words a customer would use
("chocolate, toasted almond, a little cherry"), not trade jargon. Short sentences. No exclamation marks in headlines.
We never say "artisanal", "crafted with love" or "elevate".

## Look

- **Colours:** Kestrel Rust `#B4532A` (the accent; buttons, highlights, one thing per page), Roast `#2B1D16` (text,
  dark backgrounds), Crema `#F3E9DC` (light backgrounds), Sage `#7A8B6F` (secondary), Sky `#9DB7C9` (charts only).
- **Charts:** Rust for the series that matters, Roast and Sage for the rest, Sky for comparisons. No 3D, no pie charts
  with more than four slices.
- **Fonts:** headlines in Georgia Bold, everything else in Avenir Next (Regular and Demi Bold). Both are on every Mac.
- **Logo:** `logo.png` (mark and name, on Crema), `logo-dark.png` (on Roast), `logo-mark.png` (the kestrel alone).
  Leave space around it equal to the mark's height; never stretch or recolour it.
- **Photos:** `photos/`, made for us; use them freely. Warm light, real textures.
